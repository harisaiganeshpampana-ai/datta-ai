Questions

Is Datta free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 25 messages a day, and you can try it without an account. When you need more, paid plans start at ₹399 a month — pay monthly or yearly with UPI, cards or net banking.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account your chats stay in your browser. With a free account they're saved and show up on all your devices.

Can it really build a full website?

Yes — from a one-page site to a project with a backend and database. Datta writes every file, shows you a live preview, and then guides you to publish it on GitHub, Render or Vercel with your own domain.

What can I upload?

PDFs, Word documents, text and code files, spreadsheets saved as CSV, screenshots and photos — including photos taken with your camera.

Does voice work on my phone?

Yes, in Chrome on Android and in Chrome or Edge on computers. Your browser will ask for microphone permission the first time.

Is my data private?

Your chats are only visible to you. You can delete chats, memory or your whole account at any time from Settings. Don't share passwords or bank details in chats.

What will you build first?

Start a chat. It takes seconds.
